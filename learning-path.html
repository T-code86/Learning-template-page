HOW TO USE THIS ROADMAP

THE FOUR STAGES

From the first tag
to your own work.

Move on when you can explain what you made and why it works—not when a calendar tells you to.

  1. 01

    STRUCTURE / HTML

    Give the page a foundation.

    Write a real document with semantic landmarks, useful headings, descriptive links, and images that have thoughtful text alternatives.

    Your deliverableA single article page that still makes sense with styles turned off.
    Open the HTML guide ↗
    Ready to move on?

    You can explain the purpose of the main HTML elements on your page and navigate its links with a keyboard.

  2. 02

    PRESENTATION / CSS

    Make it clear at every size.

    Build a visual hierarchy, apply a consistent spacing system, and create a layout that adapts to both narrow and wide screens.

    Your deliverableA responsive article or magazine layout with readable type and real focus states.
    Open the CSS guide ↗
    Ready to move on?

    Your content reflows without clipping or horizontal scrolling, and the visual hierarchy is easy to follow.

  3. 03

    BEHAVIOR / JAVASCRIPT

    Add interaction with purpose.

    Use JavaScript to respond to an event, update the interface, and explain the result. Keep the feature small enough to test.

    Your deliverableAn accessible filter, menu, or checklist that works with pointer and keyboard input.
    Open the JavaScript guide ↗
    Ready to move on?

    You can describe the state, event, and outcome in your feature and test the interaction without a mouse.

  4. 04

    CRAFT / YOUR PORTFOLIO

    Present the work honestly.

    Choose a project, polish the details, write a useful README, and deploy a demo. Explain what you made, what you learned, and what you would improve.

    Your deliverableA public project page with a working demo, source code, setup notes, and your own explanation.
    Choose a project brief ↗
    Ready to share?

    A visitor can understand the project, find the code, run it, and see which work and decisions are yours.

A SUSTAINABLE STUDY LOOP

Progress is
made by returning.

Choose a small task you can finish, save a working version, and write down one thing you understand better now. Revisit that note when you return to your code.

↻

When you get stuck

Describe what you expected, what happened, and the smallest step that reproduces the problem. Read the console and browser tools carefully; avoid copying fixes you cannot explain.
